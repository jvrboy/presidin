   607→  1106→  1106→which filters out minor price noise and highlights only significant trend changes. When a
   608→  1107→  1107→new swing point is identified, the system evaluates RSI behavior at that point for
   609→  1108→  1108→divergence patterns. A divergence occurs when price action and the oscillator move in
   610→  1109→  1109→opposite directions, signaling a potential shift in momentum and an upcoming reversal.

   614→  1113→  1113→stop-loss and take-profit distances, ensuring that exits adapt to current market

   616→  1115→  1115→sensitivity with specificity, filtering out noise while capturing genuine trading
   617→  1116→  1116→opportunities.

   619→  1118→  1118→late Asian session and the early London session, a period that often exhibits clean
   620→  1119→  1119→directional moves ideal for this type of momentum-based strategy.
