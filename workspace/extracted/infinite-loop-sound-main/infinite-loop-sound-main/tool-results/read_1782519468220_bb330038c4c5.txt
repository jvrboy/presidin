  1099→  1099→The ZigZag + RSI Divergence Strategy is a price-action-driven trading system that
  1100→  1100→combines swing point detection from the ZigZag indicator with momentum divergence
  1101→  1101→signals from the Relative Strength Index (RSI). The strategy is designed to identify
  1102→  1102→high-probability reversal and continuation setups during a specific trading window
  1103→  1103→aligned with the South African Standard Time (SAST) session, specifically between
  1104→  1104→00:00 and 05:00 SAST, which corresponds to 22:00-03:00 UTC.
  1105→  1105→At its core, the strategy detects structural swings in price using the ZigZag indicator,
  1106→  1106→which filters out minor price noise and highlights only significant trend changes. When a
  1107→  1107→new swing point is identified, the system evaluates RSI behavior at that point for
  1108→  1108→divergence patterns. A divergence occurs when price action and the oscillator move in
  1109→  1109→opposite directions, signaling a potential shift in momentum and an upcoming reversal.
  1110→  1110→The system is further enhanced with Stochastic Oscillator and MACD as confluence
  1111→  1111→filters. These secondary indicators confirm the strength and direction of the signal,
  1112→  1112→reducing false entries. Average True Range (ATR) is used to dynamically calculate
  1113→  1113→stop-loss and take-profit distances, ensuring that exits adapt to current market
  1114→  1114→volatility. This multi-indicator approach provides a robust framework that balances
  1115→  1115→sensitivity with specificity, filtering out noise while capturing genuine trading
  1116→  1116→opportunities.
  1117→  1117→The SAST trading window was specifically chosen to capture the overlap between the
  1118→  1118→late Asian session and the early London session, a period that often exhibits clean
  1119→  1119→directional moves ideal for this type of momentum-based strategy.
