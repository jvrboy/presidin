   198→2. The Two Rules Tested
   199→The analysis tested exactly two rules, designed to be the simplest possible approaches that
   200→any trader can execute without any specialized knowledge, indicators, or complex setup.
   201→Both rules operate on the same SAST night session window of 00:00 to 05:00 (22:00 to 03:00
   202→UTC), which spans six hourly candles. The session starts when the 22:00 UTC candle opens
   203→and ends when the 03:00 UTC candle closes. No trades are carried beyond this window,
   204→eliminating overnight gap risk and ensuring every position is flat before the London open.
   205→Rule A: First Candle Direction
   206→At 23:00 UTC (01:00 SAST), look at the candle that just closed (the 22:00 UTC hourly candle).
   207→If it closed higher than it opened (green/bullish candle), you BUY at the open of the next
   208→candle. If it closed lower than it opened (red/bearish candle), you SELL. You then hold the
   209→position until 03:00 UTC (05:00 SAST) and close it regardless of profit or loss. This is the
   210→absolute simplest possible rule: one candle determines your direction, and you trade it once
   211→per day. There is no confirmation step, no waiting, no discretion required. You either trade or
   212→you do not, based on a single visual signal that takes less than five seconds to assess each
   213→day.
   214→Rule B: Two-Candle Confirmation
   215→This rule adds one layer of confirmation to Rule A. At 00:00 UTC (02:00 SAST), you look at the
   216→last two candles: the 22:00 UTC candle and the 23:00 UTC candle. If BOTH candles agree on
   217→direction (both green or both red), you enter a trade in that direction at the open of the next
   218→candle. If they disagree (one green, one red), you sit out entirely. The position is then
   219→managed with a fixed take-profit and stop-loss, or closed at 03:00 UTC if neither level is hit.

   488→1. Strategy Overview
   489→The Squeeze Breakout strategy is one of the most reliable ultra-simple price action patterns for
   490→BTC/USD trading. It works by identifying moments when the market compresses into a tight range,
   491→indicated by candles with very small bodies relative to their total range. These periods of low activity are
   492→almost always followed by a decisive move in one direction, and by entering in the direction of the
   493→breakout candle, you capture that momentum with a high probability of success. This pattern is especially
   494→powerful during low-liquidity hours when institutional money is absent, because the few participants who
   495→are active tend to create these compression patterns that resolve cleanly without interference.
   496→The core concept is simple: when 3 consecutive candles all have small bodies (body less than 35% of the
   497→candle range), the market is compressing energy. The very next candle that closes in a clear direction is
   498→your signal. You buy if that candle closed higher than it opened, or sell if it closed lower. There are no
   499→indicators, no moving averages, no oscillators. Just raw price action. This strategy was discovered by
   500→backtesting over 8,700 hourly candles of real BTC/USD data from Deriv's public API, spanning from
   501→June 2025 to June 2026, with cross-validation on 30-minute and 4-hour timeframes confirming the
   502→pattern holds across multiple time horizons.
   503→2. The Rules (Ultra Simple)
   504→This strategy has only three rules. No thinking, no analysis, no decision-making. If the setup appears, you
   505→take the trade. If it does not appear, you wait. There is no room for interpretation or emotional bias. The
   506→rules are binary: either all three conditions are met, or they are not. This is what makes the strategy so
   507→effective for daily routine trading. You can check your chart once per candle, and in less than 10 seconds
   508→you know whether there is a trade or not. There is no need to stare at charts all day or monitor multiple
   509→timeframes simultaneously.
   510→Step-by-Step Setup Identification
   511→Step 1: Look at the last 3 closed candles on your 1-hour chart (BTC/USD). For each candle, calculate the
   512→body size (the distance between the open and close prices) and the total range (the distance between the
   513→high and low prices). Divide the body by the range to get the body ratio. If all 3 candles have a body ratio
   514→below 0.35 (meaning the body is less than 35% of the total candle range), the squeeze condition is met.
   515→Candles that meet this condition look like they have very long wicks (shadows) and tiny real bodies. They
   516→are often called doji-like or indecision candles, and when 3 of them appear in a row, it signals strong
   517→market compression.
   518→Step 2: Wait for the next candle (the 4th candle) to close. This is your signal candle. Check its direction:
   519→if the close price is higher than the open price, this is a BULLISH signal (you will BUY). If the close
   520→price is lower than the open price, this is a BEARISH signal (you will SELL). The direction of this candle
   521→tells you which way the compressed energy is releasing, and that is the direction you trade.
   522→Step 3: Enter the trade immediately when the next candle opens (the 5th candle after the squeeze). Set
   523→your Take Profit at 0.20% above your entry (for a BUY) or 0.20% below (for a SELL). Set your Stop
   524→Loss at 0.15% below your entry (for a BUY) or 0.15% above (for a SELL). This gives you a 1.33:1
   525→reward-to-risk ratio. Walk away and let the trade play out. Do not move your stops or targets. The trade
   526→will typically resolve within 1-6 candles.

  1098→1. Strategy Overview
  1099→The ZigZag + RSI Divergence Strategy is a price-action-driven trading system that
  1100→combines swing point detection from the ZigZag indicator with momentum divergence
  1101→signals from the Relative Strength Index (RSI). The strategy is designed to identify
  1102→high-probability reversal and continuation setups during a specific trading window
  1103→aligned with the South African Standard Time (SAST) session, specifically between
  1104→00:00 and 05:00 SAST, which corresponds to 22:00-03:00 UTC.
  1105→At its core, the strategy detects structural swings in price using the ZigZag indicator,
  1106→which filters out minor price noise and highlights only significant trend changes. When a
  1107→new swing point is identified, the system evaluates RSI behavior at that point for
  1108→divergence patterns. A divergence occurs when price action and the oscillator move in
  1109→opposite directions, signaling a potential shift in momentum and an upcoming reversal.
  1110→The system is further enhanced with Stochastic Oscillator and MACD as confluence
  1111→filters. These secondary indicators confirm the strength and direction of the signal,
  1112→reducing false entries. Average True Range (ATR) is used to dynamically calculate
  1113→stop-loss and take-profit distances, ensuring that exits adapt to current market
  1114→volatility. This multi-indicator approach provides a robust framework that balances
  1115→sensitivity with specificity, filtering out noise while capturing genuine trading
  1116→opportunities.
  1117→The SAST trading window was specifically chosen to capture the overlap between the
  1118→late Asian session and the early London session, a period that often exhibits clean
  1119→directional moves ideal for this type of momentum-based strategy.
  1120→2. ZigZag Indicator Settings
  1121→The ZigZag indicator is a foundational component of this strategy. It operates by
  1122→connecting swing highs and swing lows on a price chart, filtering out price movements
  1123→that do not exceed a specified percentage threshold. The ZigZag Threshold is set to
  1124→2.5%, meaning that a price move must be at least 2.5% from the last swing point to
  1125→register as a new swing. This threshold was determined through a systematic
  1126→optimization process across all 22 instruments and 5 timeframes.
  1127→A lower threshold value would produce more swing points and more signals, but with
  1128→increased noise and a higher likelihood of false entries. Conversely, a higher threshold
  1129→would generate fewer signals but with greater significance and reliability. The 2.5%
  1130→setting strikes an optimal balance, producing enough signals for meaningful trade
  1131→frequency while maintaining the quality needed for a high win rate.
